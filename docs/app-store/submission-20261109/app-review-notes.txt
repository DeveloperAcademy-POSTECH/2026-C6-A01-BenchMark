Guest experience
On the home map, tap “체험하기” (Try It), select “6냥이오이”, and tap “스토리 보기” (Read Story). To try AR, tap “수집하기” (Collect), then “시작” (Start). Allow camera access on an AR-capable iPhone. Find three cucumbers and tap “체험 카드 모으기” (Save Sample Card). No sign-in, donation, NFC tag, or campus visit is required for this sample. The sample is separate from the account's collection.

Account features
Use the review login provided in the private Sign-In Information section. From the map, tap “+” and “오이 만들기” to draft a story with an example photo and request publication. An operator approves public visibility; a donation is not required to draft or request review. To report or block, open the public test story identified in the review notes and tap “신고 및 차단”. To delete a test account, go to “MY” → “설정” → “회원 탈퇴” and confirm with the same sign-in provider. Provide the test story only after the operator has published it and the report path has been checked.

App Clip
Use the verified invocation URL recorded for this build. In the App Clip, tap “이야기·AR 체험” and follow the 6냥이오이 path above. A local developer NFC/QR test is not a public invocation URL for App Review. Record the actual invocation in the dedicated App Clip fields after the build is selected.

Donations
The app does not accept payments. Optional donations go through POSTECH’s official website. A sample story does not accept a donation.
